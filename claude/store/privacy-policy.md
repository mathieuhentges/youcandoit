# Privacy Policy — You Can Do It!

_Last updated: November 2026_

You Can Do It! is a to-do list app. It is built to keep your data on your device.

**What the app stores.** Your tasks, categories and settings are stored only on your phone.
The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
The developer cannot see your data.

**Backups.** If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the
app's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account
settings, not by the app.

**Permissions.**
- *Notifications*: to show the reminders you set.
- *Alarms & reminders*: to show reminders at the exact time you chose.
- *Run at startup*: to register your reminders again after the phone restarts.

**Sharing.** The app does not share any data with anyone.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings,
deletes everything it stored.
