# Play Console content — "You Can Do It!"

## Store listing
**App name:** You Can Do It!

**Short description (≤ 80 chars):**
Prioritized to-do lists with deadlines, reminders and a record of what you got done.

**Full description:**
You Can Do It! keeps your tasks in order and shows you how much you get done.

• Five priority levels, each with its own color, so the urgent things stand out
• Deadlines, with optional reminders: at the deadline, an hour or a day before
• Group tasks into your own categories, or see everything on the Home screen
• Tick tasks off and they stay checked at the bottom until you archive them
• History shows what you finished over the last 7 days, 30 days or all time
• Light and dark themes
• Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking
